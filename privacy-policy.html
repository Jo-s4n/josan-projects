Bouncy Bat - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Bouncy Bat ("the Game") is a casual arcade game developed by Jo-san. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Bouncy Bat.

Information We Collect

Bouncy Bat does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. The Game does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Game Data

Your game score and best score are stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

Third-Party Services

Bouncy Bat does not currently use third-party analytics, advertising services, social networks, or other third-party services that collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

Bouncy Bat does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Data Security

Because Bouncy Bat does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal information stored on our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Bouncy Bat, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the game's Google Play Store listing.
